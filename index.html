About GAODETEC

Engineered for the World's Toughest Jobs

From bridge construction and high-rise buildings to offshore platforms and nuclear facilities, GAODETEC hydraulic jacks deliver precision, safety, and reliability at every lift.

Our factory produces a comprehensive range of hydraulic equipment: synchronous lifting jacks up to 2000 tons, self-locking CLL jacks for sustained load holding, hollow-core RRH jacks for cable-stayed applications, multi-strand post-tensioning systems, and specialized flange jacks for press integration. Every unit is pressure-tested at 125% rated load and ships with full certification.
